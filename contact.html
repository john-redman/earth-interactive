Contact

Questions, corrections and ideas are all welcome. EarthInteractive is made by one person, so replies can take a few days.

Get in touch: GitHub issues

Helpful details to include

  • A wrong fact: the country, the number you saw and a source for the right one.
  • A border or name: which border view you were using (UN, De facto or Neutral) and what you think should change. Border views follow written rules, so explaining why helps.
  • A bug: your device and browser, what you did and what happened. A link from the address bar shows exactly what you were looking at.
  • A leaderboard name to remove: the display name and the date you played.

Teachers and press

Using EarthInteractive in class, or writing about it? Get in touch the same way. You are welcome to link to the globe, to any country page or comparison, and to use screenshots.

Want to help keep it free? Support the project on Ko-fi.
