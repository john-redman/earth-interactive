How to play

Everything works with a mouse, a finger or the keyboard. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

Open the globe

Move around

  • Spin: drag the globe. Let go mid-drag and it keeps turning; grab it again to stop.
  • Zoom: scroll, pinch, or press + and -.
  • Keyboard: the arrow keys turn the globe. R or Home flies back to the start view.
  • Recenter: the round target button in the bottom-right corner does the same.

A short tap or click selects a country. Anything longer or further counts as a spin, so you never open a card by accident.

Look up a country

  • Tap a country to drop a pin with its name and flag.
  • Choose Info for the full card: capital, population, area with world rank, density, GDP, languages, currency and neighbours. Tap a neighbour to jump to it.
  • Press / or Ctrl + K (⌘ + K on a Mac), or use Search, to find a country by name or capital.
  • Esc closes the card, then the pin.

Prefer reading? Every country also has its own page in the country list.

Compare true sizes

  1. Select a country and choose Compare.
  2. Tap a second country.
  3. Both lift off the globe as puzzle pieces at their true size. Drag either piece anywhere on the globe.
  4. Open Compare stats for a side-by-side table: population, area, density, GDP and more.

Just want to move one? Select it and choose Move, or press and hold it, and carry it anywhere: over the equator, up to the Arctic, next to your own country. Tap another country while it is lifted to turn it into a comparison.

Try a few classics: Greenland and DR Congo, the United Kingdom and the United States, Australia and the United States. More in the comparison list.

Border views

The switch in the top-left corner changes how disputed borders are drawn:

  • UN: borders as recognised by the United Nations and its member states.
  • De facto: who actually administers each area on the ground today.
  • Neutral: no side taken. Every contested area is shown separately, in grey.

Colour the world by data

Open Data to colour every country by population, people per km², GDP per person or land area. A legend shows what each colour means. Choose Countries to go back to the normal map.

Games

Daily Challenge

Five countries, the same for everyone in the world that day, from easy to hard. Find each one on the globe. Afterwards, copy your result as a grid of squares to share with friends. A new challenge starts at midnight, your local time.

Find it

Ten rounds. The countries start large and get smaller. Play as often as you like and try to beat your best score.

Scoring

  • The right country scores 1,000 points.
  • A miss scores less the further away you tapped. About 1,000 km off still earns around 500 points.
  • Hint tells you the region but halves the points for that round. Show me skips it.

Jump straight in with today's Daily Challenge or a round of Find it.

Sound

Flick the globe really hard and see what happens. If it gets too loud, the speaker button in the bottom-right corner mutes all sound.

Links, offline and install

  • The address bar always links to what you see: a country (?c=FRA), a comparison (?compare=FRA,DEU) or a game (?play=daily). Copy it to share.
  • After your first visit the globe also works offline.
  • Add it to your home screen from your browser's menu to use it like an app.
