Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Short version: there are no accounts, we don't ask for your name or email, and the globe keeps your game progress on your own device.

Who we are

EarthInteractive is a website run by an individual developer, John Redman (“we”). For anything about this policy, contact us via GitHub issues.

What stays on your device

The globe saves a few small settings in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you use the leaderboard (see below).

  • ei-muted: whether you turned the sound off.
  • ei-best: your best Find it score.
  • ei-daily-…: your Daily Challenge result for each day you played, so you can't replay it and can share it.
  • ei-player: a random ID and, if you chose one, a display name for the leaderboard.

To delete them, clear this site's data in your browser settings. A service worker also keeps a copy of the site's files so it loads fast and works offline. This is a cache of our own files, not data about you.

Leaderboard (optional)

When the leaderboard is switched on, you can submit a game score. We then store:

  • the random player ID from your device (not linked to your identity),
  • the display name you choose, which is shown publicly next to your score,
  • your score, the game, the rounds you played and how long it took, with the date.

Please don't use your real full name or anything that identifies you as a display name. Names are filtered for offensive words. Your IP address is used briefly to stop abuse (rate limiting) and is not stored. To remove your name and scores, contact us with your display name and the date you played.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It sets no cookies and collects no personal data. It records the page visited, the referring site, the browser and operating system, screen size and approximate country (derived from the IP address, which is not stored). We also count a few anonymous events, such as a country being opened, a comparison started or a Daily Challenge played, so we know which features people use. We can't identify you from any of this. Read GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page, and may keep it in logs for security; see the GitHub privacy statement. The globe page loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when it loads; see Google Fonts privacy.

Advertising

The site does not show ads at the moment. If and when ads are shown, they will be served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may then use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure them, based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for consent first, through a Google-certified consent message, and can change their choice at any time. Without consent, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. This policy will be updated before any ads go live.

Children

EarthInteractive is for a general audience of all ages. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The site works fully without the leaderboard. If you believe a child has submitted personal information as a display name, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or Californian law), you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we have no accounts, the only data that can relate to you is a leaderboard entry. Contact us and we will help.

Mobile app

If you use an EarthInteractive app for iOS or Android, its store listing describes the data it handles. The app may show ads through Google AdMob and will ask for consent where the law requires it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Important changes, such as turning on ads, will be noted here before they take effect.
